What a GEO Checker Does

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) audits how LLM answer engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude) ingest and cite web content. Rather than indexing keywords, AI engines extract factual triples, entity graphs, and structured JSON-LD schemas.

What Your 0–100 Score Means

0–39: Invisible to AI (Crawlers blocked or zero machine-readable entities).
40–69: Partially Indexed (Standard crawl, but no answer-first modules).
70–89: AI Citation Ready (Verified Schema, unblocked bots, clean data).
90–100: #1 GEO Authority (Curated llms.txt, full entity graph, high citation density).

The 88% Citation Disconnect

Traditional search algorithms rank pages using historical backlinks and domain age. Generative engines re-rank pages in real-time based on factual consensus, entity clarity, and whether answers can be cleanly synthesized into 2-sentence conversational citations.

Frequently Asked Questions About GEO

AI search engines prioritize pages with unblocked crawler permissions (GPTBot, PerplexityBot), clear question-based heading structures (H2/H3), direct 40-word answer paragraphs, and comprehensive JSON-LD schemas. When a query is made, the LLM retrieves relevant modular chunks and synthesizes citations from domains with high factual consistency.
An llms.txt file is a standardized markdown document located in your website's root directory (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/llms.txt). It provides reasoning LLMs with a clean, high-signal, noise-free blueprint of your core products, services, organization details, and entity links, eliminating HTML clutter and increasing citation likelihood by up to 300%.
